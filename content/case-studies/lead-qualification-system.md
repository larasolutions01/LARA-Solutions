## The situation

In property, timing matters. When enquiries arrive from many channels, the best prospects can be lost in the noise.

## How intelligence helps

Every enquiry is captured, acknowledged and qualified automatically. Agents start their day with a prioritised list rather than an inbox.

## What makes it work

- One place where every enquiry lands.
- Criteria the agents helped define.
- Follow-up that feels personal, even when it is automated.
