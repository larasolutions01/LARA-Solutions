## The situation

Managers want to run their sites, not build spreadsheets. Yet without timely data they are forced to make decisions on instinct or on information that is a week old.

## How intelligence helps

Automation removes the assembly work entirely. Short narrative summaries turn a dashboard into a briefing — “bookings at one site are below their recent average” — so attention goes straight to what changed.

## What makes it work

- KPI definitions agreed once and applied everywhere.
- Delivery where managers already work: inbox or chat.
- A clear owner for data quality.
