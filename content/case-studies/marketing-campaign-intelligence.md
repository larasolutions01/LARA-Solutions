## The situation

When every channel reports its own success metrics, budget decisions become a debate rather than an analysis. Leadership wants to know what is actually generating business.

## How intelligence helps

By joining campaign data to CRM outcomes, the team can follow prospects from first interaction to opportunity. AI-assisted segmentation then highlights the audience characteristics most associated with progression, informing targeting and messaging.

## What makes it work

- A single, agreed funnel definition.
- Disciplined campaign tagging.
- A recurring review where insights become budget decisions.
