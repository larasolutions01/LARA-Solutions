## The situation

Inventory decisions balance two risks: running out and disappointing customers, or holding too much and tying up cash. Spreadsheet planning struggles with seasonality and the sheer number of product–location combinations.

## How intelligence helps

Forecasting models handle the volume, while planners focus on the exceptions and on information the data can’t see — a new contract, a promotion, a supplier change.

## What makes it work

- A measured baseline, so improvement is visible rather than assumed.
- A planner review step that captures local knowledge.
- Alerts that surface the few items needing action today.
