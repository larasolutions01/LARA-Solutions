## The situation

Customer expectations for fast answers keep rising, but hiring support staff in line with order growth is expensive and slow to scale. Many questions — *“Where is my order?”*, *“How do I return this?”* — have clear, policy-based answers.

## How intelligence helps

An AI assistant can handle these well-defined questions immediately, at any time of day, using only approved information. When a request falls outside its remit, it passes the case to a human agent along with a summary, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.

## What makes it work

- **Guardrails first.** The assistant answers only from approved content and connected systems, and declines rather than guesses.
- **Humans in the loop.** Sensitive cases are routed to people by design.
- **Continuous review.** Conversations are reviewed regularly to improve content and routing.
